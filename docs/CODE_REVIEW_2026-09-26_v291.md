## 1. Executive Summary

Batch 75 (v291) executes an architectural review of `:feature-x` non-UI search autocompletion models, parser routines, preview transformers, and player script analyzers. It adds rating validation and immutable rating mutators (`isValidRating`, `withRating`) to `Keyword`, profile URL resolvers, subscriber validation, and video quantity formatting (`profileUrl`, `hasValidSubscribers`, `formatVideos`) to `Pornstar`, verification checks and profile link resolvers (`isVerified`, `profileUrl`) to `Channel`, consolidated suggestion listings and multi-entity query filters (`allSuggestions`, `filterByQuery`) to `SearchResult`, dedicated entity extraction helpers (`parseJsonPornstars`, `parseJsonChannels`, `parseJsonTotalCount`) to `parseJson.kt`, suggest query and URL validators (`isValidSuggestQuery`, `isSuggestUrl`) to `getSearchResults.kt`, high-definition and MP4 extension validators (`isHighQualityPreview`, `hasMp4Extension`) to `parserVideoPreviewFromImageUrl.kt`, model presence checkers and total tag counters (`hasModelsOrPornstars`, `countTotalParsedTags`) to `parserItemVideoTags.kt`, and single-item ID extractors and primary stream selectors (`parseFirstVideoIdOrNull`, `extractPrimaryStreamUrl`) to `parserListVideo.kt` and `parseHTML5Player.kt`.
